description: "Thumbs up or down on a reply, and a satisfaction rating at a moment you choose"

Feedback lets the shopper tell you how the conversation went. There are two kinds. Message feedback is a thumbs up or down under an [assistant reply](/components/conversation/assistant-reply). The CSAT survey is a card that asks for an overall rating, at the moment in the conversation you choose. Both feed the success metrics you read in [Quality and governance](/quality/overview).

## What it contains

| Part | What it holds |
| ---- | ------------- |
| Thumbs up and down | Two controls under a reply. One tap records the vote. |
| Reason chips | Shown after a down vote, such as **Not accurate**, **Not helpful**, or **Wrong product**. The shopper picks one or more, and can add a short comment. |
| CSAT survey card | A five-point rating from **Awful** to **Excellent**, with a face that changes as the shopper moves the slider, an optional **Tell us more** comment, and a **Submit feedback** button. |
| Thank-you state | A short confirmation that replaces the controls once the shopper responds. |

You choose the reason chips offered after a down vote. Each vote and each rating is tied to the reply or the conversation it belongs to, and to the Agent that answered.

## Where it appears

- **Message feedback** sits under assistant replies, next to the reply's other actions.
- **The CSAT survey card** opens over the conversation, at the moment you configure.

Both appear in every [placement](/components/widget/placements) of the widget. Down votes and their reasons point to the replies worth reviewing. CSAT ratings roll up by Agent and by Journey, next to each Journey's goals, in [Quality and governance](/quality/overview).

## When to ask for CSAT

You decide where in the conversation the CSAT card appears. Pick one or more moments:

| Moment | Asks the shopper |
| ------ | ---------------- |
| Conversation ends | When the shopper goes quiet after a finished exchange |
| Goal reached | After a Journey goal, such as an add to cart or a resolved question |
| Handoff ends | After a person on your team closes the conversation |
| Chat closed | When the shopper closes the widget |

The timing can also be dynamic. You tell the Agent in its [operating procedure](/agents/goal-and-operating-procedure) when a conversation counts as done, such as once an order question is answered, and it asks at that point. Either way, a shopper is asked at most once per conversation, and you can space it out further, such as once a week.

## Rules

Message feedback carries no rule. It shows under replies when you turn it on for the experience. The CSAT card follows the moments you choose above, not a page or a condition.

## Example

A shopper rates the conversation after the Agent answers their question.

```yaml
Ask CSAT: When a Journey goal is reached, or when the Agent judges the conversation done
Scale: Five points, Awful to Excellent
Comment: Optional
Frequency: Once per conversation
Feeds: CSAT in Quality and governance
```
