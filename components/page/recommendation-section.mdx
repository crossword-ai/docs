title: "Recommendation section"
description: "A product collection with a contextual explanation, placed on a merchant page"

A recommendation section is a product collection paired with an explanation of why these products were picked, placed on a merchant page. The shopper sees several products and a sentence that ties them to what they said, did, or arrived from, such as "Picked for your trip". The explanation is what separates it from a plain list of products.

## Examples

Every scene below is a recommendation section on a sneaker brand's storefront. The rule decides where it lands and who sees it; the embedded collection decides what is inside.

### Out of the box

The Default Playbook's section sits on the product page and replaces the storefront's generic "You may also like" carousel, for every shopper. The heading reads **Pairs well with**, three product cards show socks, insoles, and a running cap, each with image, price, and an **Add to cart** button, and the explanation reads "Chosen for the shoe you are viewing." No conditions, priority 0.

### With a rule: trail shoes in the cart

> Rule: cart page, below the cart items, insert. Cart contains a product in the Trail category. Priority 10.

A shopper with a pair of trail shoes in the cart sees a section under the items headed **Complete your trail kit**, with merino trail socks, a set of gaiters, and a pair of insoles. The explanation reads "Chosen to go with the trail shoes in your cart." A shopper with road shoes in the cart does not match, so the cart page stays as it ships.

### With a rule: VIP customers with a known size

> Rule: home page, below the hero, insert. All of: member of "VIP customers", preferred size is known. Priority 20.

A VIP shopper whose size is on their profile opens the home page and sees **New arrivals in your size** under the hero, with four cards filtered to that size and the explanation "Everything here is in stock in a size 9.". Both conditions must hold. A VIP shopper with no size on file does not match, and sees the home page as it ships.

### With a rule: product out of stock

> Rule: product page, add-to-cart area, replace. Product on the page is out of stock. Priority 30.

On a sold-out pair, the add-to-cart area is replaced by a section headed **In stock now, same feel**, with three alternatives that share the pair's cushioning and drop, and the explanation "Closest matches to the pair on this page, all shipping today.". The disabled add-to-cart button is gone, so the shopper has a way forward instead of a dead end.

### With a rule: sale weekend

> Rule: cart page, below the cart items, replace. Scheduled window 28 to 30 November. Priority 15.

For three days, the section under the cart items reads **Sale picks under 50** with four discounted products and the explanation "Marked down this weekend only.". At priority 15 it outranks the trail kit section at 10 for the same placement, so a trail shopper sees the sale picks during the window and the trail kit again on Monday.

### What else a rule can do

- Show picks tied to a campaign for shoppers who arrived with a specific UTM campaign.
- Recommend alternatives only after the shopper has viewed two products in a row.
- Show a two-card carousel on mobile and a four-card grid on desktop.
- Skip the section once a shopper has dismissed it, for 14 days.
- Exclude shoppers who already have one of the recommended products in the cart.
- Wait 10 seconds after the page loads before the section appears.
- Target every page under `/collections/` with picks for that collection.
- Cap the section to once per session on the cart page.

Every condition, combination, timing option, and delivery limit is listed in [Rules](/orchestration/rules). The shared rule shape is under [Page sections](/components/page/page-sections).

## Anatomy

| Property | What it holds |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Heading | The title above the section, such as **Picked for your trip**. Supports dynamic variables. |
| Product collection | The embedded [product collection](/components/cards/product-collection): the product cards, their ordering, optional filters, and a carousel or grid layout. |
| Contextual explanation | Copy that explains the selection in terms of what the shopper said, did, or arrived from. Supports dynamic variables. |

The products can be a fixed set you choose, or picked at display time from what Crossword knows about the shopper and the page. Each product card keeps its own identity and events, so you can follow a shopper from the section being shown to a card being clicked to an add to cart.

## Where it appears

- **Product page.** Alternatives to the product on the page, or items that complete it. Below the description or near the add-to-cart button.
- **Cart page.** Items that go with what is already in the cart.
- **Home and collection pages.** Picks for a returning shopper, or for a visitor who arrived from a campaign.

The section goes into a placement. It can be inserted at a location, or replace an existing part of the page such as a generic "You may also like" block. Read [Pages and placements](/orchestration/pages-and-placements).

## Rules

A recommendation section carries its own rule, scoped by its target page and placement. Target page, placement, and placement action are required. Conditions, priority, timing, and delivery limits are optional.

The rule decides whether the section shows. The ordering and filters on the embedded collection shape what is inside it.
