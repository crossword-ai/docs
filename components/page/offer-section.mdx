---
title: "Offer section"
description: "An offer, who is eligible for it, an explanation, and a call to action"
---

An offer section shows a shopper an offer, tells them whether they qualify, explains it, and gives them one action to claim it. A bundle discount on the product page, or free shipping on the cart page when the total is close to the threshold. It puts the offer at the point where the shopper is deciding, instead of in a banner they scroll past.

## Examples

Every scene below is an offer section on a sneaker brand's storefront, first with no rule and then with a different rule each time.

### Out of the box

The Default Playbook's section sits on the cart page, inserted above the checkout button, for every shopper. The offer reads **Free shipping over 75**, the explanation uses a dynamic field to say how much is left, such as "Add 18 more and shipping is on us.", and the button reads **Find something under 20**. Tapping it opens the widget with that prompt already sent. Once the cart passes 75 the copy switches to "Free shipping applied." No conditions, priority 0.

### With a rule: one pair in the cart

> Rule: cart page, above the checkout button, insert. Cart contains exactly one pair of shoes. Priority 10.

A shopper with a single pair in the cart sees **Second pair, 20% off both** in place of the free-shipping offer, with the explanation "Add any second pair and save 20% on both. Applied automatically at checkout." and a single **Add a second pair** button. Tapping it opens the widget with "Show me a second pair to go with my cart" already sent. The placement takes one section, and 10 beats 0.

### With a rule: VIP customers

> Rule: cart page, above the checkout button, insert. Member of "VIP customers". Priority 20.

A VIP shopper sees **Members save 15% on everything** in the same spot, with the explanation "Your member discount is applied at checkout. Free shipping on every order." and an **Apply and checkout** button. At priority 20 this outranks the second-pair offer, so a VIP with one pair in the cart sees the member offer and not the bundle.

### With a rule: sale weekend

> Rule: home page, promotion banner, replace. Scheduled window 28 to 30 November. Frequency cap once per session. Priority 15.

For three days, the storefront's own promotion banner on the home page is replaced by an offer section reading **Sale weekend: 25% off site-wide**, with the explanation "No code needed. Ends Sunday at midnight." and a **Shop the sale** button. Outside the window the rule is not a candidate and the storefront's banner shows. The frequency cap keeps the section from reappearing on every return to the home page in one session.

### With a rule: arrival from a campaign

> Rule: product page, near the add-to-cart button, insert. UTM campaign is `unboxing-a`. Suppress once claimed. Priority 10.

A shopper who follows the link from an unboxing video sees **Video viewer bonus: free socks with this pair** next to the add-to-cart button, with the explanation "Added to your cart automatically when you buy this pair." and a **Claim the bonus** button. Once the shopper claims it, suppression removes the section for the rest of the session, even though the UTM condition still matches.

### What else a rule can do

- Show a first-order discount to shoppers with zero lifetime orders.
- Offer a volume discount when the cart holds three or more of the same product.
- Show a mobile-only offer with a device condition.
- Wait 20 seconds of inactivity on the cart page before the offer appears.
- Exclude shoppers who already used the offer code.
- Serve one offer to any of several UTM sources.
- Hold an offer back for 7 days after the shopper dismisses it.
- Target every page under `/collections/sale` with a matching offer.

Every condition, combination, timing option, and delivery limit is listed in [Rules](/orchestration/rules). The shared rule shape is under [Page sections](/components/page/page-sections).

## Anatomy

| Property | What it holds |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Offer | The offer itself, such as a bundle discount, free shipping, or a gift with purchase. |
| Eligibility | Who the offer applies to, such as a segment, a cart value, or a product in the cart. |
| Explanation | Copy that describes the offer and any conditions. Supports dynamic variables, such as the amount left to reach free shipping. |
| Call to action | The button or link that claims or applies the offer, such as **Apply discount**. |

Eligibility is a property of the offer: who it is for and what the storefront honors. The rule on the section decides who sees the section at all. The two can match, or differ when you want a shopper to see what they could unlock, such as free shipping a little further up the cart.

## Where it appears

- **Cart page.** The most common spot. Above the checkout button, or next to the totals.
- **Product page.** Near the add-to-cart button, for a bundle or a volume discount.
- **Home and collection pages.** A campaign offer for visitors who arrived from a specific source.

The section goes into a placement. Insert is the usual action, though it can replace an existing promotion block. Read [Pages and placements](/orchestration/pages-and-placements).

## Rules

An offer section carries its own rule, scoped by its target page and placement. Target page, placement, and placement action are required. Conditions, priority, timing, and delivery limits are optional.

For an offer that unfolds over time, such as a reminder after a cart is abandoned, use a [Campaign](/orchestration/campaigns) instead.
