---
title: "Placements"
description: "Where the widget sits on your storefront: a middle bar, a floating widget, or a sidebar"
---

You choose where the widget sits on your storefront. The conversation inside is the same in every placement. Only its position and how it opens change. Each image shows the closed state on the left and the open state on the right.

## Middle bar

A chat bar centered at the bottom of the page. It opens into a chat window in the middle of the page.

<Frame caption="Middle bar, closed and open">
  <img src="/images/widget/placement-middle-bar.png" alt="Middle bar, closed and open" />
</Frame>

## Floating widget

A launcher in the bottom right corner, with conversation starters stacked above it. It opens into a chat window above the launcher. You can move it to the bottom left.

<Frame caption="Floating widget, closed and open">
  <img src="/images/widget/placement-floating.png" alt="Floating widget, closed and open" />
</Frame>

## Sidebar

The same launcher and starters when closed. It opens as a full-height panel along the right edge of the page, and the page stays visible beside it. You can open it on the left edge instead.

<Frame caption="Sidebar, closed and open">
  <img src="/images/widget/placement-sidebar.png" alt="Sidebar, closed and open" />
</Frame>

You set the placement in the widget's [customization](/components/widget/overview#customization). It applies to every page of the experience.
