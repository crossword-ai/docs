title: "Product card"

A product card shows one product and lets the shopper act on it without leaving the conversation. It is the most common card in Crossword, and every product collection is built from it. Its job is to turn a mention of a product into something the shopper can see, pick a variant of, and add to the cart in one step.

## Example

A shopper on a sneaker storefront asks the widget "Do you have the trail runner in a wide fit?" and there is one clear match. The reply carries a single product card: the product image on top, the title with a one-line description and a **New** badge, the price with the original price struck through beside it, and an "In stock" line. Below that sits a size selector with the wide sizes as chips, one already selected, and two buttons, a filled **Add to cart** and a text **View details**. Tapping **Add to cart** adds that variant to the storefront cart without leaving the chat.

## Anatomy

| Property | What it holds |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Product info | The title, a short description, and any badges such as **New** or **Best seller**. |
| Image | The product image. |
| Price | The current price, and the original price when the product is discounted. |
| Availability | Whether the product is in stock, low in stock, or out of stock. |
| Variant selection | A [selector](/components/primitives#selector) for size, color, or other options, so the shopper can pick a variant on the card. |
| Buttons | A primary call to action such as **Add to cart**, plus secondary actions such as **View details** or **Compare**. Built from the [button](/components/primitives#button) primitive. |

Product data comes from your catalog. The card renders whichever product the Agent, or the component that included it, selects. You do not write a card per product.

## Where it appears

A product card never shows on its own. One of these rule-based components includes it, and that component's rule decides when the shopper sees it.

- In a [welcome message](/components/conversation/welcome-message), for example the product a shopper arrived to see from a video or an ad.
- In a [proactive message](/components/conversation/proactive-message), for example a nudge on a product page that shows the item with its size selector ready.
- In the [assistant reply](/components/conversation/assistant-reply), where the Agent chooses it. The Agent shows one card when there is a single clear match and a [product collection](/components/cards/product-collection) when there are several.
- On a page, inside a [recommendation section](/components/page/recommendation-section), for example three add-ons on the cart page. On a page the card usually arrives as part of a collection rather than alone.

The card is also the building block of other components. A [product collection](/components/cards/product-collection) is a set of product cards. A [comparison card](/components/cards/comparison-card) shows each compared product as a card. A [bundle builder](/components/interactive/bundle-builder) uses product cards for the candidates in each product group.

## Rules

A product card carries no rule. It shows because a welcome message, a proactive message, or a page section with a rule included it, or because the Agent composed it into the assistant reply. The rule that decides whether and when the shopper sees it lives on that component, not on the card. Read [Rules](/orchestration/rules).
