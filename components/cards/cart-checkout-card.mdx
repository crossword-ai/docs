---
title: "Cart and checkout card"
description: "The shopper's basket, totals, and checkout controls"
---

The cart and checkout card shows the shopper what is in their basket and lets them change it or check out from inside the conversation. As a cart card it focuses on items and quantities. As a checkout card it focuses on the summary and the controls that lead to payment. Either way, the shopper finishes without leaving the chat.

## Example

A shopper with two pairs in the basket types "What's in my cart?" and the reply shows the cart card. Each line item has a thumbnail, the title, the chosen size and color, a quantity stepper, a **Remove** link, and the line price on the right. Below the items sit the subtotal, a discount line from an applied code, and the total, then one add-on suggestion, a pair of running socks as a small product card. A filled **Proceed to checkout** button closes the card. Tapping the stepper changes the storefront cart at the same time, so the cart page shows the same count on refresh; in checkout mode the same card collapses the items to a summary and leads with the payment controls.

## Anatomy

| Property | What it holds |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Items and quantities | Each product in the cart with its variant, image, and quantity. |
| Pricing and totals | Line prices, subtotal, discounts, and the total. |
| Modifications | Controls to change a quantity or remove an item. |
| Recommendations | Optional products suggested alongside the cart, such as an add-on for an item already in it, shown as [product cards](/components/cards/product-card). |
| Cart or checkout controls | The buttons the shopper can use, such as **Proceed to checkout**. Only the controls your storefront permits are shown. |

The card reads the live cart from your storefront. When the shopper changes a quantity on the card, the storefront cart changes with it.

## Where it appears

A cart and checkout card never shows on its own. One of these rule-based components includes it.

- In a [welcome message](/components/conversation/welcome-message), for example when a returning shopper opens the widget with items still in their cart.
- In a [proactive message](/components/conversation/proactive-message), for example a cart recovery message that shows the items left behind. The [cart recovery Campaign](/use-cases/cart-recovery-campaign) sends one at each step of its sequence.
- In the [assistant reply](/components/conversation/assistant-reply), where the Agent chooses it. The shopper asks what is in their cart, asks to remove something, or asks to check out, and the Agent answers with the card.
- On a page, inside a [page section](/components/page/page-sections) on the cart page, for example an [offer section](/components/page/offer-section) that shows the basket beside the discount that applies to it.

## Rules

A cart and checkout card carries no rule. It shows because a welcome message, a proactive message, or a page section with a rule included it, or because the Agent composed it into the assistant reply. The rule that decides whether and when the shopper sees it lives on that component, not on the card. Read [Rules](/orchestration/rules).
