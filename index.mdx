Crossword is an AI shopping assistant for your storefront that greets shoppers, answers their questions, recommends and sells products, and resolves support issues. You organize it into Journeys, one for each situation a shopper is in, and Agents, the assistants that answer inside each Journey.

## How the docs are organised

The navigation has nine groups. **Getting started** explains the model and how it fits together. **Components** covers everything a shopper can see. **Journeys** covers the situations that decide what shows, and **Agents** covers who answers. **Global settings** holds the defaults every Journey and Agent share. **Targeting and campaigns** covers who sees what, where, and over time. **Quality and governance** covers how you measure and improve. **Setup and publishing** covers channels and going live. **Example journey** walks through a complete setup.
