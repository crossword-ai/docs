title: "Try Crossword on your store"
description: "See your Crossword Agent on your own store before going live, using the Chrome extension on desktop or the iOS app on iPhone. Your shoppers see no change."

You can see your Agent on your real store without adding anything to the site. Your Crossword team sets up your store for testing, so you only need to install Crossword on your device. It appears only on the device you set up, so your shoppers see no change.

## On iPhone

You need an iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    <p style={{ margin: 0 }}>Install Crossword: Brand's AI Agent.</p>
    <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/crossword-brands-ai-agent/id6828818194" style={{ display: "block", width: "fit-content", borderBottom: "none", textDecoration: "none" }}>
      <img noZoom src="https://toolbox.marketingtools.apple.com/api/badges/download-on-the-app-store/black/en-us" alt="Download on the App Store" style={{ display: "block", height: "48px", width: "auto", marginTop: "8px", marginBottom: 0 }} />
    </a>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the extension">
    Go to **Settings > Apps > Safari > Extensions > Crossword** and turn on **Allow Extension**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow website access">
    Follow the instructions in the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your store">
    Open your store in Safari. The Agent appears on the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If the Agent does not appear

- **Reload the page.** A page opened before you installed Crossword needs a reload.
- **On iPhone, check website access.** Go to **Settings > Apps > Safari > Extensions > Crossword** and confirm the extension is on and website access is allowed.
- **Contact your Crossword team.** If the Agent still does not appear, ask them to confirm your store is set up, and share the exact address you are testing.
