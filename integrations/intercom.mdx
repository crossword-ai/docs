Once you connect Intercom, every chat your Agent hands to a person goes to Intercom instead of Crossword's Inbox. Your support agents reply from Intercom as usual. The visitor gets the replies in the Crossword widget.

Intercom connects through a **private app** you create in your own Intercom.

<Note>
  Only workspaces hosted in the **United States** can be connected for now.
</Note>

To learn when your Agent hands off to a person, read [Escalation and handoff](/agents/escalation-and-handoff#human-handoff).

## Before you start

You need admin access to your Crossword workspace and to Intercom.

Open **Helpdesk > Connection** in the [Crossword dashboard](https://app.crossword.ai). This guide calls it the Helpdesk connection page.

## Connect Intercom

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the app in Intercom">
    In Intercom, go to **Settings > Developers > [Developer Hub](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/_/developer-hub)** and create a new app. Choose an internal or private app.

    In the app, copy its **access token**.

    In the same app, copy its **client secret**. Crossword uses it to check that updates really come from Intercom.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect in Crossword">
    On the Helpdesk connection page, choose **Intercom**.

    Pick the data region **United States**, then enter the **access token** and **client secret**.

    Click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the app in Intercom">
    Crossword shows an **endpoint URL**, the **topics** to tick, and the **permissions** to grant. Make these changes to your app in the [Developer Hub](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/_/developer-hub), in this order.

    Under **Configure > Authentication**, give the app exactly these permissions:

    - Read and write conversations
    - Read and write users and companies
    - Read and write tags
    - Read and write tickets
    - Read admins

    Under **Configure > Webhooks**, paste the **endpoint URL** Crossword shows.

    Tick the **topics** Crossword lists, then save. Intercom sends a test notification, and the Crossword page shows webhooks as working once it arrives.

    Under **Basic information**, set the app's **API version** to the one Crossword shows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After you connect

- The **Health** section on the Helpdesk connection page shows whether the access token and webhooks are working.
- If the access token stops working, use **Replace the access token**. Open chats stay where they are.
- Turn on identity capture in the widget, so handoffs land under the visitor's email. Your Agent can also ask for it.
